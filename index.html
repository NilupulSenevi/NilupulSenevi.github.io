About

Building robots that grasp, fly, and learn

I'm a Master of Science graduate now pursuing a PhD in Functional Control Systems at the Shibaura Institute of Technology, Tokyo — where I also earned the Electrical Engineering and Computer Science Award. My work centers on robotic manipulators, drone-mounted robot arms, image processing, and object detection, with a growing focus on imitation learning and AI-driven control.

Outside the lab, I've designed and built industrial robot arms, quadruped robots, custom grippers, and UGVs, and have a long-running interest in unmanned aerial vehicles. I'm a skilled software developer in Python, C++, Arduino and Java, an academic reviewer for IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, and a robotics instructor for the next generation of engineers.
